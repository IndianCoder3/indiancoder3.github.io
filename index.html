Who am I?

About Me

Hey! I'm Abhinu, known online as @IndianCoder3. I'm a 6th-grade student from India. While I attend school like everyone else, I've taught myself programming languages like Python and many other technologies on my own. I love building across the entire stack: from bare-metal OS kernels in C, to polished web apps, to AI-powered tools.

My most ambitious projects are AbhinuOS and the upcoming Andex OS — custom operating system kernels that have taught me more about how computers really work than anything else. Andex OS will eventually ship with a full desktop environment.

I also built Basic ICTL (my favourite project), a web IDE with Monaco + Emmet, a Windows clock app in .NET, a QR code generator in Streamlit, and particle graphics in Visual Basic. I've been active on Scratch for years with over 100+ projects, which is where my coding journey really started.

I am fluent in English and Hindi. When I'm not in school or coding, I'm probably experimenting with new tech. You can find me sharing my work and experiments on GitHub and YouTube as @IndianCoder3.
